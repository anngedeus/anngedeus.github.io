Conducted a comparative design and content audit of marketing materials and legal disclosures across commercial AI mental health chatbots.

Coded promotional claims and formal disclaimers to identify representational dark patterns, risks of therapeutic misconception, and ethical concerns related to user vulnerability and informed consent, applying frameworks from deceptive design and sociotechnical systems to examine how language, framing, and placement shape user trust and perceived clinical authority.
