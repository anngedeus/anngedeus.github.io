summary: Investigating how positive self-talk can be supported in young adults through non-clinical AI companions.
date: 2024-08-01
tags:
  - Human-AI Interaction

---

Designed and conducted a qualitative study with 11 licensed therapists and 4 AI experts to investigate how positive self-talk (PST) can be supported in young adults through non-clinical AI systems.

Developed the Human-AI Self-Talk Framework (HAIST) to translate therapeutic strategies into interaction design principles for AI companions, and performed qualitative coding and thematic analysis to surface insights relevant to young adults.
